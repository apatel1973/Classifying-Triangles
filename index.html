Classify a Triangle

One triangle. Two ways to describe it.
Read the numbers and look closely at the shape.

Need a reminder?

Acute: every angle is less than 90°. Right: one angle is 90° (look for the square). Obtuse: one angle is greater than 90°.

Scalene: all sides are different. Isosceles: two sides match. Equilateral: all three sides match.

Equal angles face equal sides. When only sides are labeled, use the drawing to decide the angle type.

Both answers must be correct to earn a point. Take your time!
